About VERTEX

With over 15 years of excellence in luxury interior design, Anantha Design Group has been transforming spaces into timeless masterpieces. Our philosophy combines classic elegance with contemporary innovation.

We believe that every space tells a story. Our team of award-winning designers works closely with clients to create environments that reflect their unique vision, lifestyle, and aspirations.

From residential sanctuaries to commercial landmarks, we bring unparalleled expertise, attention to detail, and a commitment to exceeding expectations in every project we undertake.
